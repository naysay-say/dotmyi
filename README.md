# dot my i

A focused video player for macOS — mark moments, capture frames, 
and export to your editing workflow.

## Support

For questions or feedback, please contact:  
dotmyi.support@gmail.com

## Features

- Pin markers on any moment in a video (⌘D)
- Capture any frame as a screenshot (⌘⇧S)
- Load external subtitles (.srt, .ass, .ssa)
- Export markers as FCPXML (Final Cut Pro) or EDL (Premiere Pro)
- Export clips as GIF, APNG, or MP4

## Privacy Policy

dot my i does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.  
All videos and notes remain entirely on your Mac.

## System Requirements

macOS 14 or later
